## Scope

HQ dashboard (public/dashboard.html, src/live/dashboard.ts) and the Scout 4 phone page (public/phone.html, src/live/phone.ts). Mode: Operate. Laptop screen viewed up close by 2 to 4 judges; phone held up at about a metre. Code-led (no image generation).

## Audience and task

Neptune North judges read within seconds who is cut off, for how long, and what they did meanwhile. ElevenLabs and n8n judges see their tools at work. The team operates it live: radio on, cut n8n link, watch devices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HQ's knowledge drawn as a storm-warning chart: communication failure is weather moving over the map, with one monumental line saying what is happening now. Refuses the grid of status cards and the dark neon ops console.

OWN-WORLD: night chart. Deep navy ground #0a1433, floods as soft merged shapes, a quiet dot grid, rooftops as raised blocks that fade when a drone is over them. Craft drawn from above in near-white: quadcopter drones for scouts, rescue boats for rescuers, IDs inside. Neptune periwinkle radio links with travelling packets and a soft coverage glow; red crosses on cut links. Yellow, amber and red only for communication warnings and cut links, pink only for an isolated actor. Barlow and Barlow Semi Condensed, signage-cut, tabular numerals for every count and tick.
Ground history: the first build used a cool pale ground. The user rejected it ("it looks messy and bad ... it can look much much better and more sleek than this"); the dark night chart is Claude's response to that, not yet explicitly confirmed by the user.

STORY: the judge sees six actors on one map sharing one goal; links cut and messages route around; a team goes dark inside an amber warning zone and keeps working as ghosts on stated intent; on reconnect the zone clears, the backlog lands, conflicts settle, and the result is compared with central and independent control.
